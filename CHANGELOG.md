# Changelog

What changed in Grimwilder, one entry per merged pull request, grouped by the day it merged (UTC), newest first.

## 2026-11-09

- **Journal pages fold open on your sheet**: tap a page's title on the Journal card to read it there, with Edit below to change it. ([#108](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/108))
- **The journal uses the whole screen**: pages sit side by side, all the same height, and the GM's list of journals spreads out too. ([#108](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/108))
- **Sort your journal**: favorites first, chronologically, or last updated. ([#108](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/108))
- **Star a page as you write it**, and stars sit neatly beside their titles. ([#108](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/108))
- **Talent uses in the log are the GM's call**: a new setting, Post talent uses to the table log, puts every use a character spends in the log. It replaces the checkbox each player had on their sheet. ([#107](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/107))
- **Talents to take show on your sheet**: when your level allows more talents (or Eldritch Talents) than you have, the Talents card's title says how many, even folded, and tapping it opens the talent picker. ([#107](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/107))
- **Scene collections**: scene blocks are now called scene collections, laid out two across on the prep page, each with its scenes and its buttons at the bottom. Scenes in no collection are standalone scenes. Every card folds, with Expand all and Collapse all at the top, and Add a scene opens its form right in the card. ([#107](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/107))
- **Move scenes from a menu**: each prepared scene has Edit, Move and Remove; Move lists the other collections. ([#107](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/107))
- **Starting a scene shows everything you've prepared**: each collection's scenes, then the standalone ones, then a new scene. Sessions no longer ask where the players are headed. ([#107](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/107))
- **Your scene collection in progress comes first**: the collection the last scene came from leads the prep page and the scene picker, open and marked In progress; the others start closed. ([#107](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/107))
- **Cut to a new scene without leaving the table**: ending the scene, calling a montage for it, and starting the next all happen in pop-ups over the GM table. ([#107](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/107))
- **Scenes open with a story question**: give a scene a story roll question, and starting it opens the story roll with the question filled in, so you pick the odds and who rolls. Until it's rolled, the question waits on the table with Roll it. ([#107](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/107))
- **Journals are pages you keep as you like**: New page opens a form right in the journal or on your sheet, with a title if you want one, whenever you want. Pages no longer belong to a session; the ones you'd written keep their session as their title. Tap a page's title to read it, change it or tear it out. ([#105](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/105))
- **Lists in the chat and journals**: start lines with `- ` for bullets or `1. ` for numbers. ([#105](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/105))
- **Star your favorite pages** to keep them at the top of your journal and your sheet. ([#105](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/105))
- **Character Secrets**: every player's journal starts with a starred page that you and your GM both write in. ([#105](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/105))
- **The GM can add pages to a player's journal**, from the journal or the character's sheet: a secret, a backstory. They're read only for the player unless the GM says otherwise, and the GM sees them even in a private journal. ([#105](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/105))
- **Fold away the sheet's cards**: every card on a character sheet but the stats and harm opens and closes from its title, and remembers how you left it. Talents come first, then What looms, Journal, Arcana, Story and Who; What looms, Journal, Arcana and Story start closed. ([#104](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/104))
- **What looms shows a count** of the pools looming over the table, like a notification badge. ([#104](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/104))
- **Your journal on your sheet**: write about this session right from your character's sheet, see your last page, and open every journal from the card. Your GM sees it there too while you share it. ([#104](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/104))
- **Who they are, and their story, edit in place**: the sheet splits who a character is (backgrounds, traits, desires, features, gear) from their story (bonds, arcs, notes), and each card has its own Edit. They're no longer on the Edit sheet page. ([#104](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/104))
- **Pages use more of a wide screen**: the margins at the sides are a quarter of what they were on a tablet or wider, so the talent catalog, sheets and tables spread wider. ([#103](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/103))
- **The table chat takes a third of the page** beside the character sheet, the GM table and the campaign page on a wide screen, instead of a narrow column. On a wide screen these pages fit the window: the header and footer stay put, the page scrolls on its own, and the chat fills the height with Send always in view. ([#103](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/103))
- **A roomier Taken list** when you choose talents: wider, with each talent's name, its summary and a Remove button in place of the ✕. ([#103](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/103))
- **The path picker fills more of the screen**, with less space around it. ([#103](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/103))
- **Talents follow Community Edition 1.0**: Flurry, which the book no longer has, is gone, and every talent's page number now points to the right page in the book. Settings now give the right pages for the Wealth, Milestone levels and Focused growth optional rules. ([#101](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/101))
- **Pick your path from cards**: making or editing a character, Pick your path opens a list of every path with its tagline and core talent, and the talent's full text a tap away. New characters no longer start as a Fighter. ([#100](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/100))
- **The Warlock's core talent is Pact again**, as the Community Edition's book calls it. Saved characters keep it. ([#98](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/98))
- **Trap Making is back among the Artificer's talents**, with the book's text (no spark when a trap goes off). ([#98](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/98))
- **The Taken list shows each talent's summary** under its name when you choose talents, so you can compare your picks without scrolling back to their cards. ([#98](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/98))
- **Path taglines follow the book** for the Druid, Monk, Paladin, Ranger, Summoner, Swashbuckler and Wizard, the Fighter is also known as a warrior, and Spellcraft and Audacity's tip match its wording. ([#98](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/98))

## 2026-11-05

- **Admins can remove an account** from Admin › Accounts. Its characters go, and so does any campaign it was alone in; its rolls and chat stay in the log as a removed account's. It can't be your own account, or the only GM of a campaign others are in. ([#96](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/96))
- **A welcome email for the interest list**: once someone who asked to try Grimwilder has an account, they're emailed once to say it's ready and how to sign in. People given accounts before now get theirs too. ([#96](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/96))

## 2026-11-03

- **Safer deploys**: only a release made by the site's admin can deploy, and the deploy's GitHub Actions are pinned to exact versions. ([#92](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/92))
- **Tidier sheet controls**: spark, thread and XP are the same width on your character sheet, and Edit sheet is an outlined button. ([#91](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/91))
- **A menu on phones**: below tablet width the header is just the wordmark and a ☰ menu holding the tabs, Feedback and your account, so pages start higher up the screen. ([#90](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/90))
- **The chat fills the width** whenever it sits below the page (phones, tablets, narrow windows), instead of staying a narrow column. ([#90](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/90))
- **Jump to the chat on a phone**: where the table chat sits below the page (the sheet, the GM table, the campaign page), a Chat button in the corner takes you straight to it, ready to type. It hides once the chat is on screen. ([#90](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/90))
- **Easier to tap on a phone**: bigger, better-spaced talent tick boxes; full-height spark, thread, XP and suspense buttons; pool rows that keep Roll and ⋯ together when a long name wraps; and tidier lines on the GM table and the roll setup. ([#90](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/90))
- **Archive a character**: put a character away from the ⋯ on their card or their edit page. They move to an Archived list at the foot of your Characters page, out of play (and out of any table they were playing at) until you unarchive them. ([#89](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/89))
- **Archived campaigns get their own list** at the foot of the Campaigns page, with Unarchive for the GM, instead of sitting among the ones still playing. ([#89](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/89))
- **Campaigns still playing come first** in a character's campaign dropdown, before archived ones. ([#89](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/89))
- **Long campaign names fit**: the campaign dropdown on a character's card no longer runs outside the card; a long name is cut short on the button and shown whole when the list opens or on hover. ([#89](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/89))
- **Polls**: from the GM table's new "Share with the table" fold, the GM asks the players a question, written answers or a set of choices. Each player answers in a pop-up on whatever campaign page they're on, and only the GM sees the answers, live, until the poll is closed. ([#88](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/88))
- **Handouts**: the GM shares an image (a map, a letter, a diagram) with an optional caption. It lands in the table chat, and only people at that table can open it. ([#88](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/88))
- **Feedback**: an admin can turn on a Feedback button for an account; it sends what they think along with the page they were on. Admins read it under Admin › Feedback and can copy it all as Markdown for an AI. ([#87](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/87))
- **Searchable dropdowns**: every dropdown is drawn in Grimwilder's own style, and long ones (time zones, paths, talents) have a search box. They work from the keyboard too. ([#86](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/86))
- **Early access**: new accounts start on the Early access plan, with no limits on how many campaigns they run or play in. ([#85](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/85))
- **Scene blocks**: group your prepared scenes by where the players might go ("The castle"), each block with its own notes. Start a session on a block and its scenes come first; any prepared scene can still be pulled into any session. ([#85](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/85))
- **A new way to start a scene**: from the GM table, a modal lists this session's block, the loose scenes and the other blocks; start one as written in one tap, adjust it first, or make a new one. ([#85](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/85))
- **Running clocks** on the GM table: how long the session and the current scene have run, ticking beside the scene's title. ([#85](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/85))
- **Scene notes at the table**: while a scene is on, its notes (and its block's) show in the GM table's scene bar. ([#85](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/85))
- **Journals**: each player keeps a journal at a campaign, a page per session or for the stretch between, with *italics* and **bold** as in the chat. The GM can read it unless the player keeps it private. ([#85](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/85))

## 2026-11-02

- **Chat opens on Table talk** on the GM table and on character sheets; the campaign page still shows everything. ([#84](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/84))
- **Releases deploy**: merging no longer ships the site; publishing a GitHub release does, and admin's Site stats shows the release running. ([#84](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/84))

- **On hold**: when a plan runs fewer campaigns than you have, the most recently played keeps going and the rest wait, with chat and sheets still open. Choose which to play from the campaigns list. ([#81](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/81))
- **Done is now Archived**: an archived campaign stays to read, and nothing new is played there. ([#80](https://github.com/phChun/grimwilder/pull/80))
